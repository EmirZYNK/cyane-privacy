Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

Welcome to Cyane: Photo Editor & Effects ("we", "our", or "us"). We are deeply committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring you have complete control over your creative media. This Privacy Policy outlines how your data is handled when using our iOS application.

Summary: Cyane processes all your photos and edits directly on your device. We do not upload, store, analyze, or share your personal photos with external servers.

1. Photo & Camera Access

To apply analog film filters, grain effects, and custom templates, Cyane requires access to your device's photo library. All photo processing, filter matrix rendering, and export calculations occur 100% locally on your iOS device. Your images are never transmitted to our servers or third parties.

2. In-App Purchases & Subscriptions

Cyane offers auto-renewable subscriptions (Cyane Pro). All financial transactions are securely processed by Apple via the App Store. We do not collect or store your payment details, credit card numbers, or billing addresses.

We use RevenueCat to manage subscription validation and restore operations anonymously. RevenueCat may process anonymous user identifiers to verify purchase status. For more details, visit RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

3. Data Collection & Analytics

We do not collect personal information such as your name, email address, physical location, or browsing habits. We do not use third-party tracking cookies or advertising SDKs.
